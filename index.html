Shipping & Delivery Process

Every fish is carefully prepared using a professional live-animal shipping system to ensure safe arrival.


1. Health Inspection

Each fish is checked for activity, color quality, and feeding before packing. Only healthy fish are shipped.

2. Fasting Preparation

Fish are fasted for 24 hours to reduce waste during transit and keep water clean.

3. Oxygen Packing

Fish are sealed in double-layer oxygen bags with temperature-stable water conditions.

4. Heat & Insulation Control

Insulated boxes and heat/cold packs are added depending on weather conditions.

5. Secure Boxing

Packages are shock-proof sealed to prevent movement and stress during transportation.

6. Overnight Shipping

Orders are shipped via fast courier services for next-day delivery whenever possible.

7. Live Arrival Guarantee

If a fish arrives dead on arrival (DOA), we offer replacement or refund upon proof within 1 hour.

8. Customer Support

We guide you after delivery with feeding, acclimation, and tank setup instructions.
