🚚 Shipping Process

Safe & Professional Fish Shipping

Every fish is carefully prepared using industry-standard live animal shipping procedures to ensure it arrives healthy, stress-free, and ready for its new home.

🩺
Step 01

Health Inspection

Every fish undergoes a complete health inspection. Only active, healthy fish are selected for shipment.

🍽️
Step 02

Fasting Preparation

Fish are fasted for 24 hours to reduce waste and keep water clean during transit.

💨
Step 03

Oxygen Packing

Fish are sealed in oxygen-filled double bags with temperature-controlled water.

🌡️
Step 04

Temperature Protection

Heat or cold packs are added to maintain stable temperature during shipping.

📦
Step 05

Secure Packaging

Insulated and shock-proof packaging prevents stress and movement.

🚚
Step 06

Express Shipping

Orders are shipped using fast courier services for next-day delivery when possible.

✅
Step 07

Live Arrival Guarantee

If a fish arrives DOA, we offer replacement or refund upon proof within 1 hour.

💙
Step 08

Customer Support

We provide full guidance on acclimation, feeding, and aquarium setup after delivery.
